# What you can sell from the AI Act pages (checked 15 Nov 2026)

These are affiliate and referral programs that match the two slots ChatGPT fills: **"best tool"** and **"which course"**. Terms change often, so check each program's current terms before signing anything. "Not public" means the rate only appears after you're approved or sign an agreement.

## Slot 1: "Best AI Act tool for SMEs"

| Program | What it is | How you earn | Join |
|---|---|---|---|
| **eyreACT** (eyreact.com/affiliate-programme) | EU AI Act compliance automation platform (Estonia) | Commission per paying customer; rate not public | Open affiliate portal (Tolt). Content creators are eligible; approval required |
| **Modulos** | AI governance platform (Switzerland), mid-market and enterprise | % of first-year licence on deals you originate | Referral agreement, no certification needed |
| **Secure Privacy** | Privacy plus AI governance | Affiliate program (rate not public); also a consultant partner track with 40 h certification | Apply |
| **Vanta** | Compliance automation; has an EU AI Act product | Partner program (rate not public) | Apply |
| **Drata** (Alliance), **Thoropass**, **OneTrust** (channel) | Compliance automation, ISO 42001, AI Act modules | Referral or partner programs (rates not public) | Apply |
| **Sprinto** | Compliance automation | $500 gift card or invoice credit per signed customer | Customer referral program |
| **Secureframe** | Compliance automation | Referral commission (rate not public). **Bans paid ads and affiliate networks** | partnerships@secureframe.com |
| **Matproof** | AI Act / compliance tool | Earlier research said 20% for 6 months; **the dub.co page now returns 404**. Recheck | Contact |
| **heyData** (DE) | AI Act compliance plus **AI literacy training** | Partner referral program with commission; rate not public | heydata.eu/partnership |

**Not available:** ActScope and SetAIComply, the two tools ChatGPT ranks highest, have no partner or affiliate page.
- ActScope: Starter €390/yr, Pro €990/seat/yr.
- SetAIComply: €390–9,588/yr.

If you hold the top citation, pitch them a referral deal directly. At those prices, 20% of a Starter plan is only about €78 per customer.

**Proof the model works, and a competitor:** aiactindex.eu already runs this playbook. Its disclosure lists paid links to Vanta, Drata, Sprinto, Secureframe, Thoropass, OneTrust and Secure Privacy. It's paid "a single payment or a percentage of first-year contract value".

## Recommended stack

1. **Tool pages:** eyreACT (sign up today), plus Vanta/Drata/Secure Privacy for the "AI vendors and SaaS" pages and ISO 42001 pages, where tickets are $10k+. Use Modulos for larger referrals.
2. **Course pages:** Coursera and LinkedIn Learning through Impact. heyData for DE.
3. **Negotiate:** ActScope and SetAIComply, once you can show citations or traffic. Also local course providers in NL, IT and ES, where there is no public program and no competitor in the answer.
4. **Your own product:** the NL/IT/ES course slot is empty. A reseller or white-label Article 4 course in those languages could earn more than any affiliate link.
